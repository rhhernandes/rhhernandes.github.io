Awards & honours

  • 2026 · European Press Prize

    European Press Prize — Innovation Award

    Laureate, with Guardian colleagues, for a year-long investigation tracing far-right radicalisation across a network of UK Facebook groups, built on analysis of 51,000 posts.

  • 2025 · University of Cambridge

    Huw Price Prize

    Best overall performance in the MPhil in Ethics of AI, Data, and Algorithms. Degree awarded with Distinction.

  • 2025 · University of Cambridge

    Harding Distinguished Postgraduate Scholarship

    Fully funded doctoral scholarship supporting the PhD at Cambridge Digital Humanities.

  • 2019 · ESET

    ESET Award for Information Security Journalism — Winner, Brazil

    For reporting on the cybersecurity debate around Brazil's electronic voting machines ahead of the 2018 election.

  • 2017 · Folha de S.Paulo

    Prêmio Folha — Winner

    For an investigation into the personal-data trail internet users leave behind. Twice more shortlisted, for coverage of ChatGPT (2023) and of platform terms of service (2017).
